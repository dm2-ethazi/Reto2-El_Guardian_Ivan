# Arte del menú

Fondo: `Assets/Sprites/Juego/FondoMenu.png`.
Generado con la herramienta integrada ImageGen; los controles son UI nativa de Unity.

## Prompt utilizado

Create a finished widescreen 16:9 background illustration for the main menu of a Spanish indie 2D pixel-art fantasy castle-defense and cipher puzzle game. NO TEXT NO LETTERS NO UI NO BUTTONS. Rich, carefully hand-pixelled retro game environment, restrained visible pixel clusters, atmospheric layered depth, polished indie game art. Composition: left 45 percent dark quiet navy woodland shadows and subtle mist, low contrast empty area suitable for overlaying title and menu. On the right half an imposing ancient blue slate stone castle in three-quarter view on a rocky hill, tall asymmetric towers with muted purple peaked roofs and purple banners, amber lit narrow windows, an arched entrance glowing subtle spectral mint green, winding cobblestone approach from bottom center-right, pine silhouettes framing edges, distant indigo mountains and drifting teal mist. Small pale golden moon high on right, starry deep navy sky. Palette midnight navy, desaturated slate blue, muted violet, restrained mint magic and warm amber lights. Dramatic yet inviting, beautiful coherent pixel-art scene, no photorealism, no smooth 3D rendering, no characters. The castle is the focal point on right, left stays very dark and understated for readable menus. Wide landscape 16:9.

## Comprobaciones

Revisión visual en Unity a 1920 × 1080. Comprobados en Play: iniciar partida, cambiar de español a euskera y volver, abrir la confirmación de salida y cancelarla. Referencias locales de la escena validadas sin ausencias ni IDs duplicados.
